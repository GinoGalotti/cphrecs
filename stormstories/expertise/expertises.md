# Expertises

*An inspiration list for Stormlight characters. Player-safe.*

Expertises are the narrow things your character simply *knows*: a culture, a trade, a weapon, a world. They sit next to your skills, not on top of them. Nothing here is a hard rule. Take an entry as written, narrow it, or invent your own and run it past the GM.

## How it works

An expertise does three things at the table:

- **Assumed knowledge.** You just know the basics, with no roll. A Thaylen doesn't roll to work out the exchange rate between marks and broams.
- **Advanced thinking.** It lets you attempt tests others can't, such as identifying a rare poison or reading an ancient glyph.
- **Expert traits.** Weapon and armour expertises unlock the item's expert trait (see the Weapons and Armour tabs).

**How you get them**

- **At creation:** 2 cultural expertises, plus a number of utility, weapon or armour expertises equal to your Intellect.
- **As Intellect rises:** each new point of Intellect gives a new expertise.
- **Downtime training:** about 20 days with a mentor, an expert to watch or a reference book (roughly 5 marks a day in supplies), then a few tests.
- **As rewards:** concluding a goal or surviving something memorable can grant one.
- **Temporarily:** from a reference text mid-adventure, or talents such as the Scholar's Erudition.

**Naming your own.** The narrower an expertise is, the more it should do in its niche. "Military Life" helps a little in many places. "Siege Logistics" might skip the roll entirely when you fortify a town. A good expertise is a subfield or a cross-section of experience, not a copy of a skill.

**Expertises and the plot die.** When the plot die comes up, your expertise is what makes the twist fit. An Opportunity for a trader might be spotting that the merchant's broams are fake. A Complication for a scholar might be a convincing text that turns out to be wrong.

**Why it matters.** Specific expertises mean everyone gets a moment where only they can move the story forward. The Warrior can't read the ancient script, and the Scholar can't find the black-market fence.

## Cultural

Every character starts with two. A cultural expertise means fluent, unaccented speech in the language, plus the customs, religion and social rules of that people. Two cultures can tell a story: an Alethi raised in Kharbranth, or a Herdazian who grew up on the caravan roads.

| Culture | You know… |
|---|---|
| **Alethi** | The lighteyes/darkeyes system and the dahns and nahns. Vorin rules on the safehand and masculine and feminine arts. Military codes and the politics of the highprincedoms. |
| **Azish** | The Azish bureaucracy: forms, essays, viziers and the Prime. The meaning of patterns and colours in Azish dress. How to petition properly. |
| **Herdazian** | The great web of "cousins". Street food like chouta. Crystalline fingernails and their uses. Living in the shadow of bigger neighbours. |
| **Iriali** | The faith of the One and the Long Trail. What golden hair and skin mean to the Iriali. Iri's trade and its neighbours. |
| **Kharbranthian** | The Palanaeum and the scholars and surgeons of Kharbranth. A city of bells and patients, where healing is offered to all. |
| **Listener** | Listener songs, rhythms and forms. Life on the Shattered Plains. *Talk to the GM before taking this one.* |
| **Natan** | The storm-battered east. Old Natan folklore and lost glory. Surviving where highstorms hit hardest and shelter is scarce. |
| **Reshi** | The island-nations built on the backs of the great Tai-na. Life at sea and in the tropics. A blunt, less formal society. |
| **Shin** | Shinovar's strange soil, grass and animals. Reverence for stone. A society that honours farmers and looks down on warriors. |
| **Thaylen** | Sea trade routes and currency exchange. The Passions. Ships, ports and merchant houses. Thaylen eyebrow styles. |
| **Unkalaki** | The Horneater Peaks. Birth-order roles within a family. The gods of the lakes, peaks and trees. Feasts and hospitality. |
| **Veden** | Jah Keved's Vorin court and the Holy Enclave in Valath. The ardentia. Noble houses and old feuds. |
| **Wayfarer** | Life on the road and in caravans. Every ford, waystation and highstorm shelter. A little of every border town's customs. |

**More cultures from the *Stormlight World Guide*:** Emuli, Tukari, Tashikki, Yezieri, Steen, Almi, Deshi, Marati, Riran, Selay (Purelake), Babatharnam, Marabethian, Yulay and Tu Bayla. Ask the GM if you'd like one of these.

| **Locksmithing & Mechanics** | Picking locks, naming who made one, disarming multi-stage traps. How non-magical machines work. | Thievery, Deduction |
| **Forgery & Counterfeiting** | Writs of passage, seals and signatures. Spotting fake spheres (glass, not gemstone). | Deception, Crafting |
| **Underworld Contacts** | Thieves, fences, smugglers and safehouses: the jargon, the signs, where to find black-market goods. | Insight, Persuasion |
| **Cryptography & Ciphers** | Writing and breaking coded messages, including hidden meaning in women's script. | Deduction |
| **Disguise & Prosthetics** | Makeup, posture and costume. Passing as another caste or trade and building a false identity. | Deception |
| **Sleight of Hand** | Palming spheres, planting evidence, cheating at cards unseen. | Thievery, Agility |
| **Poisons & Toxins** | Toxins such as blackbane. Recognising a poisoning, handling poison safely, and antidotes. | Medicine, Crafting |
| **Surveillance** | Tailing someone through a crowd, and noticing when you're the one being followed. | Stealth, Perception |

## Weapons

Anyone can swing a sword. A weapon expertise unlocks its **expert trait**, which is where trained fighters pull ahead.

| Weapon | Skill | Traits | Expert trait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knife | Light Weaponry | Discreet | Offhand, Thrown [20/60] |
| Mace | Light Weaponry | — | Momentum |
| Shortspear | Light Weaponry | Two-Handed | Loses Two-Handed |
| Sidesword | Light Weaponry | Quickdraw | Offhand |
| Staff | Light Weaponry | Discreet, Two-Handed | Defensive |
| Shortbow | Light Weaponry | Two-Handed | Quickdraw |
| Axe | Heavy Weaponry | Thrown [20/60] | Offhand |
| Hammer | Heavy Weaponry | Two-Handed | Momentum |
| Longspear | Heavy Weaponry | Two-Handed | Defensive |
| Longsword | Heavy Weaponry | Quickdraw, Two-Handed | Loses Two-Handed |
| Shield | Heavy Weaponry | Defensive | Offhand |
| Crossbow | Heavy Weaponry | Two-Handed | Deadly |
| Longbow | Heavy Weaponry | Two-Handed | Indirect |
| Unarmed | — | — | Momentum, Offhand |
| Improvised | — | — | Gains the traits of the most similar weapon |

**Tips**

- Want a spear and a shield? The Shortspear expertise removes Two-Handed.
- Defensive (Staff, Longspear) lets you Brace without cover, which suits Leaders who stand in the open giving orders.
- Unarmed expertise makes you dangerous even when you've been disarmed. Improvised suits anyone who fights with whatever is lying around.

## Armour

Armour expertise lets you wear it well: fewer penalties, or looking fit for court.

| Armour | Deflect | Traits | Expert trait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uniform | 0 | Presentable | — (crafting and repair only) |
| Leather | 1 | — | Presentable |
| Chain | 2 | Cumbersome [3] | Loses Cumbersome |
| Breastplate | 2 | Cumbersome [3] | Presentable |
| Half Plate | 3 | Cumbersome [4] | Cumbersome [3] |
| Full Plate | 4 | Cumbersome [5] | — (crafting and repair only) |

**Tips**

- Chain expertise is the cheap fix for a low-Strength character who still needs protection.
- **Presentable** means you aren't penalised in polite company while armoured, which matters in an Alethi or Azish court.

## Specialist

Rare, restricted knowledge. You can't pick these at character creation without the GM's say-so or a talent that grants one. You earn them in the story: long downtime with rare texts and mentors, or as a goal reward.

| Expertise | What it covers | Usually needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Fabrial Crafting** | The artifabrians' guarded craft: pick an effect, get the right gem, lure and trap a spren, then build it. | Crafting; gems; a teacher or a talent such as Prized Acquisition |
| **Soulcasting Protocols** | How Soulcasters coax a thing to become something else, and the Essences it can become. | GM permission; Soulcaster access |
| **Radiant Lore** | What can still be found about the Knights Radiant and their orders, beyond the usual stories of betrayal. | Rare texts, patient research |
| **Shardbearer Lore** | Shardblades and Shardplate up close: summoning, strengths and the weak points of an enemy Shardbearer. | Training with a Shardbearer |
| **Ryshadium Bonding** | Riding a Ryshadium, which chooses its rider. Without this you can't use its special abilities. | The horse's trust (a high-tier companion) |

*The GM may hold other specialist expertises in reserve. Some knowledge is something you have to find in play.*
