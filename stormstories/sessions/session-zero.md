# Stars & Wishes — Session −1 (Tutorial Mission)

*Bridge Nine · Cosmere RPG · October 2026*

After every session the table shares **stars** (what worked) and **wishes** (what we want more of). Here's what came out of our first outing on the Shattered Plains.

## ⭐ Stars

- **The split.** The group was divided right at the start and had to work its way back together. The tension gave the whole session a strong dynamic.
- **Staying in character.** Players held true to their characters' choices, even when that made things harder.
- **Camp scenes.** Small improvised details and mini-stories around the fire defined who these bridgemen are, and they're written down for later.
- **Playing off each other.** Players built on each other's ideas instead of waiting their turn.
- **Bridgemen together strong.** The crew felt like a crew.
- **The lore die.** Well used, and it rewarded curiosity about the world.
- **A welcoming table.** New-to-Roshar players felt supported in learning the world as they went.
- **Improvised GMing.** Off-script moments were handled smoothly, with a good balance of guardrails and freedom to describe your own actions.

## 🌠 Wishes

- **More space for the players.** Fewer GM descriptions and more open questions. Silence is an invitation.
- **More ambient world.** Sprinkle in spren, crem, rockbuds, and cultural details so Roshar feels alive between the big moments.

## 🛠 What changes next session

1. **Endeavours open with a question.** Expect prompts like *"How do you improve the camp?"* before anything is described. You fill the gap.
2. **Spren in every scene.** Expect two or three ambient spren per scene, tied to emotion and weather. When you see one, it's telling you something about the moment.
3. **More texture.** Crem underfoot, rockbuds opening after rain, safehands, glyphwards, chouta from street stalls, highstorm rituals.
4. **Camp stays.** Every session keeps a camp scene.
5. **Spren handout.** A spoiler-safe quick reference is on its way.

## 📚 Spren references

New to Roshar? These are safe starting points.

- **[Welcome to Roshar](https://www.brotherwisegames.com/)** (Brotherwise Games): a short illustrated booklet covering storms, spren, flora, fauna, and cultures. Spoiler-free, and the best first read.
- **Stormlight World Guide** (Cosmere RPG): the official art book, with canon visuals of spren and creatures. Ask at the table to flip through it.
