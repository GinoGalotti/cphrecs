# Before the First Storm

*Session zero primer for Stonewalkers*

Read this, think about it, and bring your answers to session zero. Half-answers are fine. You can't get any of this wrong.

---

## I. The World

1. **How close to canon?**
   - **1. Canon is sacred:** the books happen exactly as written, and we stay at the edges.
   - **3. Canon is the spine:** big events hold, but the details can bend around us.
   - **5. Our Roshar:** canon is a backdrop, and we may change it.
2. What do you love most about Roshar? What should be in every session?

## II. The Tone

3. **Lethal or heroic?**
   - **Lethal:** any fight can kill you, and injuries follow you for weeks.
   - **Heroic:** you will bleed, fail and lose, but death comes only when it matters and the story has earned it.
4. What's off the table entirely? What should happen off-screen?

## III. Who You Are

5. **Purpose:** What drives your character? Why do they get up in the morning?
6. **Goals:** Name one you could achieve soon, one that might take the whole campaign, and one dream your character would never say out loud.
7. **On the road:** We open in the Unclaimed Hills. For about a week you've been travelling toward the Shattered Plains warcamps with each other and with Taszo, an injured Shin monk. Why are you with him, and what are you leaving behind? The starting hooks at the end of this page can help.
8. In this era, most people believe the Knights Radiant betrayed humanity and the Heralds are myth. What does your character believe, and who taught them that?

## IV. The Cracks

> *On Roshar, power enters through the broken places.*

9. **Obstacle and flaws:** Name two or three real flaws. They should be things that cost your character, not cute quirks. Share them with the group, or only with me.
10. What is a painful truth your character has never said out loud?
11. What would your character trade a piece of themselves for?

**The table contract:** Flaws create friction, never an exit. Playing your cracks will be rewarded, sometimes in small ways, sometimes in ways that change who you are. Finding the reason your character stays with the group is always your job, not mine.

## V. Someone Else

12. **A second life:** Sketch someone tied to your character or the group. Give their name, how they know you, what they want, and where they are right now. They'll grow alongside the story. They may cross your path as someone you meet, or step in as your character if Roshar takes the first one. Keep it light now; we'll flesh them out if they're needed.

## VI. Each Other

*Answered together at the table.*

13. Each player picks at least one of these and asks it of another player, who answers. Explore as many as you like, or invent your own.
    - You don't trust me. What suspicious act did you witness me doing?
    - I had a moment of vulnerability with you on the road. What caused it, and why could you relate?
    - You've either woken up early with me or stayed up late with me. What have we learned about each other?
    - By coincidence, I have a friend or family member in common with you. Who is our mutual connection? How did I find this out?
    - You and I have butted heads. What was our argument about?
    - I'm fast friends with you. What pastime have we enjoyed together on our travels?
    - I have a controversial opinion about Taszo. Why do you agree with me? How have we communicated away from the others?
    - I'm fascinated by something about you or your life. What question of mine were you not ready to answer?
    - One night over dinner, I shared a cultural practice or family tradition. Did you already know the tradition? If not, what made you join in?
    - I needed a small favor along the way. Why did I approach you instead of another companion, and what did I ask you for?
    - I remind you of someone you lost. Who, and what do you do differently around me?
14. As a group: what happened on the road this week that you still talk about?

## VII. Bring a Moment

15. Think of one scene you hope we play, and one thing you'd hate to see.

## Why You Walk with Taszo

*Starting hooks from Stonewalkers. Each suggests the heroic paths it suits best. Take one, bend one, or ignore them all.*

### Agent or Hunter
Taszo mentioned his companions were killed by agents in green robes, their faces painted with strange glyphs. You can't ignore a mystery like this, so you agreed to help him.
*Why do you take a personal interest in the mysterious circumstances of the Shin monks' assassination?*
**Suggested goal:** Solve the mystery of who killed Taszo's companions and why.

### Envoy or Scholar
You're interested in the people, cultures, or religions of the world. Perhaps knowledge beyond the ways of your home country will help you make a difference on the world stage. Given the insularity of the Shin, Taszo is one of the few Shin you've met, and you've agreed to aid him and explain local Alethi culture.
*How will learning more about the world and its people help you? Why do the Shin matter particularly to you?*
**Suggested goal:** Learn about the Shin Stone Shamans.

### Agent or Leader
For the last year, you've been plagued by dreams of a dark storm, larger and more terrible than any highstorm. In the eye of the storm is a curly-haired Shin monk. A week ago, you met the monk from your dreams. He was looking for traveling companions, so you joined.
*Why can't you ignore these strange dreams? Have you shared your dreams with anyone, or do you keep them a secret?*
**Suggested goal:** Discover the meaning of your dreams.

### Leader or Warrior
You traveled with the retinue of Stone Shamans who were sent to retrieve a sacred relic. While you aren't privy to the politics or plans of the shamans, you were there when the assassins attacked and Taszo saved you, risking his own life. Now the two of you seek to complete the original task.
*What's your relationship to Taszo and your role in the retinue? Are you an acolyte from Shinovar or someone met during the journey east?*
**Suggested goal:** Repay Taszo for saving your life.

### Scholar or Envoy
Fascinated by myth and lore, your scholarly work concerns the Lost Radiants, Heralds, or Honorblades. You met Taszo while traveling to the Shattered Plains and are intrigued by his vast knowledge of secret histories.
*What set you on this path of knowledge? What lively theological debate have you had with Taszo?*
**Suggested goal:** Investigate the secrets of the Lost Radiants and the Heralds.

### Warrior or Hunter
You met a strange injured Shin monk who was looking for the services of a guide and bodyguard to see him safely to the Shattered Plains. You agreed to help him.
*Who were you before becoming a bodyguard? Why did you agree to travel with Taszo?*
**Suggested goal:** Protect Taszo and help him complete his mission.
