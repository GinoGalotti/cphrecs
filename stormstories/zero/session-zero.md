# Before the First Storm

*Session zero primer for Stonewalkers*

Read this, think about it, and bring your answers to session zero. Half-answers are fine. You can't get any of this wrong.

---

## I. The World

1. **How close to canon?**
   - **1. Canon is sacred:** the books happen exactly as written, and we stay at the edges.
   - **3. Canon is the spine:** big events hold, but the details can bend around us.
   - **5. Our Roshar:** canon is a backdrop, and we may change it.
2. What do you love most about Roshar? What should be in every session?

## II. The Tone

3. **Lethal or heroic?**
   - **Lethal:** any fight can kill you, and injuries follow you for weeks.
   - **Heroic:** you will bleed, fail and lose, but death comes only when it matters and the story has earned it.
4. What's off the table entirely? What should happen off-screen?

## III. Who You Are

5. **Purpose:** What drives your character? Why do they get up in the morning?
6. **Goals:** Name one you could achieve soon, one that might take the whole campaign, and one dream your character would never say out loud.
7. **On the road:** We open in the Unclaimed Hills. You've been travelling toward the Shattered Plains warcamps with the same companions for about a week. Why are you going there, and what are you leaving behind?
8. In this era, most people believe the Knights Radiant betrayed humanity and the Heralds are myth. What does your character believe, and who taught them that?

## IV. The Cracks

> *On Roshar, power enters through the broken places.*

9. **Obstacle and flaws:** Name two or three real flaws. They should be things that cost your character, not cute quirks. Share them with the group, or only with me.
10. What is a painful truth your character has never said out loud?
11. What would your character trade a piece of themselves for?

**The table contract:** Flaws create friction, never an exit. Playing your cracks will be rewarded, sometimes in small ways, sometimes in ways that change who you are. Finding the reason your character stays with the group is always your job, not mine.

## V. Someone Else

12. **A second life:** Sketch someone tied to your character or the group. Give their name, how they know you, what they want, and where they are right now. They'll grow alongside the story. They may cross your path as someone you meet, or step in as your character if Roshar takes the first one. Keep it light now; we'll flesh them out if they're needed.

## VI. Each Other

*Answered together at the table.*

13. Each player answers one of these about another character:
    - Who here do you owe?
    - Who here reminds you of someone you lost?
    - Whose secret do you know, or think you know?
    - Who don't you trust yet, and why are you wrong?
14. As a group: what happened on the road this week that you still talk about?

## VII. Bring a Moment

15. Think of one scene you hope we play, and one thing you'd hate to see.

*Journey before destination.*
