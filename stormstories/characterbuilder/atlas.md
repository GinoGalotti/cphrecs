# Stormlight Character Atlas

Player reference for the Cosmere RPG *Stormlight Handbook*: character creation plus every heroic, Radiant, Enlightened, and Singer talent tree. Talent summaries are paraphrased; page numbers are the Handbook's printed pages and the book is the rules authority.

**Activation icons:** ▶ 1 action, ▶▶ 2 actions, ▶▶▶ 3 actions, ▷ Free action, ↺ Reaction, ★ Special activation, ∞ Always active

## Creating a character

1. **Origins** (p. 16). Pick an ancestry (human or singer) and up to two cultural expertises. Your ancestry grants bonus talent(s), chosen in step 5.
2. **Starting path** (p. 17). Choose one heroic path: Agent, Envoy, Hunter, Leader, Scholar, or Warrior. You'll gain its starting skill rank and key talent. Radiant paths open from level 2.
3. **Attributes** (p. 18). Spread 12 points across Strength, Speed, Intellect, Willpower, Awareness, and Presence, max 3 in any one. Then note lifting capacity (STR), movement (SPD), recovery die (WIL), and senses range (AWA).
4. **Skills and expertises** (p. 18). Take 1 free rank in your path's starting skill, then 4 more ranks anywhere (max 2 per skill at level 1). Skill modifier = ranks + linked attribute. Add expertises equal to your Intellect on top of your two cultural ones.
5. **Talents** (p. 20). Take your path's key talent. Humans add one more heroic-path talent; singers take Change Form plus one forms talent.
6. **Equipment** (p. 20). Choose a starting kit, optionally spend its marks, and record your armor's deflect value.
7. **Story** (p. 21). Pick a purpose (what drives you) and at least one obstacle (what holds you back), then one or two goals with your GM. Sketch connections, personality, and appearance, and a possible Radiant order if you want one.
8. **Final calculations** (p. 23). Health = 10 + STR. Focus = 2 + WIL. Physical defense = 10 + STR + SPD; Cognitive = 10 + INT + WIL; Spiritual = 10 + AWA + PRE. Level 1, no Investiture yet.

### Ancestry

**Human** (size Medium, p. 30)
- At level 1 (step 5) take one bonus talent from any heroic path — another from your own path, or the key talent of a second path.
- Gain another heroic-path bonus talent at each new tier: levels 6, 11, 16, and 21.

**Singer** (size Medium, p. 31)
- You can take talents from the Singer tree (see the Singer tab).
- At level 1 you gain Change Form plus one of Forms of Finesse, Resolve, or Wisdom, and choose your starting form.
- At levels 6, 11, 16, and 21, take a bonus talent from the Singer tree or any heroic path.
- Forms change only during a highstorm; form bonuses are temporary and don't count toward prerequisites.

### Purpose, obstacle, goals (p. 275)

- Example purposes: Save others, Seek truth, Push yourself to new heights, Find connection, Seek justice, Preserve life
- Example obstacles: Deep regret, Specific fear, Spite, Self-doubt, Mistrust of others
- Example goals: Become a noble, Find a missing person, Fulfill a specific oath, Repay a debt of gratitude, Get revenge
- Each goal has three milestone boxes. The GM ticks one when you meaningfully advance it — usually at most once per session.
- With three milestones you can conclude the goal when the moment is right: by success, by growing past it, or even by failure.
- Concluding a goal earns a reward: a companion, a fabrial or high-quality gem, an Ideal, a patron, Shards, or a title.
- Radiant Ideals are goals too, but they come from your Ideal talents rather than the GM.

## Radiants and surges

- **Becoming Radiant** (p. 123): From level 2 you can take an order's First Ideal key talent. It grants Investiture right away and the goal “Speak the First Ideal”; the order's two surges unlock when you complete it.
- **Investiture** (p. 123): Max = 2 + the higher of Awareness or Presence. Works like focus. You start most scenes full if you carry marks worth 3× your Investiture (only 1 if Surprised).
- **Breathe Stormlight** (p. 124): Draw Stormlight from infused spheres within 5 ft. to refill Investiture. Usable while Unconscious.
- **Enhance** (p. 124): 1 Investiture: Enhanced +1 Strength and +1 Speed until the end of your next turn; maintain for 1 Investiture each turn.
- **Regenerate** (p. 124): Free action, 1 Investiture: heal 1d6 + tier. Usable while Unconscious.
- **Ideals** (p. 126): Each Ideal is a goal with three milestones earned by living your order's philosophy. When you speak the Words you become Empowered for the scene (advantage on all tests, Investiture refills each turn) and unlock the talent's reward. Fifth Ideals aren't available.
- **Breaking oaths** (p. 128): First lapse: nothing. Repeat lapses before you've made amends strip your highest Ideal and the talents that depend on it, one Ideal at a time. Rededicate yourself to repair the bond.
- **Squires** (p. 128): From the Third Ideal (Take Squire), trusted allies within 50 miles get Investiture 2, the Stormlight actions, and optionally 1 rank in your surges.
- **Your spren** (p. 129): No stat block. Moves at your speed and must stay within spren bond range (30 ft.). Covert Scouting (2 focus), Oath Encouragement (2 focus), Sudden Warning (3 focus), Translation (2 focus), plus order-specific abilities.
